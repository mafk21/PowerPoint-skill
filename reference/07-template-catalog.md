## Custom service (for reference when scoping "done-for-you" work)

- **Basic — $35 / 10 slides** (min 20 slides): custom layout, typography,
  professional visuals, ≤3 revisions, fast delivery. No transitions/animations.
- **Professional — $75 / 10 slides** (min 10 slides): structured visual
  storytelling + **Morph & smart animation** + custom animation + optional
  intro logo animation, ≤3 revisions.
- Process: order form → confirm & pay → design → revisions & final `.pptx`
  delivery (Drive/WeTransfer/email). Content writing & data-viz are add-ons.
- Their differentiation frame: *typical deck* = basic animation, inconsistent
  layout, weak storytelling, generic structure ✕ — *PlacePlate deck* = Morph &
  smart animation, structured visual storytelling, consistent design system,
  professional flow ✓. (Steal this frame for comparison slides.)
